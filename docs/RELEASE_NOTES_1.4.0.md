# Kariakoo Hustle 1.4.0 Release Notes

Android version code: `4`

Release date: 2026-08-09

## Google Play - Kiswahili

Biashara imekuwa bora zaidi! Chagua quality ya supplier, linda cash ya rent na transport, na uone break-even kabla ya kuuza. Dashboard sasa inapendekeza opportunity ya soko, ripoti zinaonyesha bidhaa zilizosababisha returns, na Mpango wa Kesho unakupa hatua tatu za kuendeleza biashara. Tumeongeza pia stock aging, mbinu za uuzaji, mission streaks, maboresho ya UI, na marekebisho ya stability.

## Google Play - English

Business just got smarter! Choose supplier quality, protect cash for operating costs, and see your break-even target before selling. The dashboard now highlights market opportunities, reports identify products causing returns, and Tomorrow's Plan gives three useful next actions. This release also adds stock aging, selling strategies, mission streaks, UI improvements, and stability fixes.

## Full Release Summary

- Added Budget, Standard, and Premium supplier-quality choices with real price and return-risk tradeoffs.
- Added product-level return and quality-loss reporting.
- Added a pre-sale break-even planner using current costs, stock margins, and property income.
- Added a two-day operating-reserve planner to stock purchases.
- Added a contextual Tomorrow's Plan with three actions after every daily report.
- Added a dashboard market brief and next-best-move guidance.
- Added Safe, Balanced, and Aggressive selling strategies with advisor recommendations.
- Added gentle stock aging and clearer clearance recommendations.
- Added mission streak bonuses and expanded business advice.
- Improved bilingual Swahili and English UI, small-screen layouts, save migration, and automated coverage.

## Tester Focus

- Buy the same product using each supplier-quality tier and confirm price and Inventory quality labels change.
- End several days and confirm returned products appear against the correct item in the report.
- Confirm Break-even and operating-reserve calculations react to loans, workers, properties, and purchases.
- Use all three Tomorrow's Plan actions and verify navigation reaches the correct screen.
- Close and reopen the app to verify save migration and supplier-quality data persist.
