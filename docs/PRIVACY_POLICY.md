# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 16, 2026

Kariakoo Hustle is an offline-friendly mobile business simulation game.

## Information We Collect

The MVP does not require an account and does not run a backend server.

The game stores progress locally on your device, including:

- Business name
- Fake in-game cash and inventory
- Game day, level, XP, reputation, achievements, reports, settings, and language preference
- Local save backup data if the player chooses to export or import progress

## Ads

The app contains technical preparation for Google AdMob rewarded ads, but ads are currently disabled in gameplay.

If ads are enabled in a future release, Google AdMob may collect or process data such as device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and advertising data according to Google's policies and the user's consent settings.

Rewarded ads, if enabled later, will be optional. The game will not include betting, gambling, cash-out, or real-money reward mechanics.

## Purchases

The current MVP does not include in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, or cash-out rewards.

## Children

The game is designed as a casual business simulation. Before release, complete the Google Play target audience and content rating forms accurately based on the final product and marketing.

## Data Sharing

The MVP does not send gameplay save data to a Kariakoo Hustle server. If ads are enabled later, ad-related data may be processed by Google AdMob.

## Data Deletion

Players can reset local progress in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes local save data from the device.
