Build a Tanzanian biashara empire from 50,000 TZS of fake capital.

## Full Description

Kariakoo Hustle: Biashara Empire is a Swahili-first business simulation game inspired by Tanzanian street-market energy.

Start with 50,000 TZS of fake capital, buy stock, sell products, manage expenses, handle random business events, and grow from one small Kariakoo table into a full biashara empire.

Learn business thinking through play:

- Buy and resell stock
- Manage cash flow, rent, transport, loans, and salaries
- Choose products by demand, risk, and profit margin
- Reinvest into upgrades, workers, locations, property, and new markets
- Handle realistic business surprises, rivals, and daily reports
- Switch between Kiswahili and English

The game is offline-friendly, lightweight, and designed for Android players. It uses fake TZS only. There is no gambling, no betting, no cash-out, and no real-money reward system.
